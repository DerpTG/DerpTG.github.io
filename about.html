Who I am

I started college as a commuter with a head start and a plan to finish in three years. That meant six classes a semester pulled from across the Penn State system, and teaching myself whatever the compressed schedule skipped over.

Security was not the plan at first. I came in for computer science generally, and it narrowed once I found the part I actually liked, which was working out what happened after the fact. My professors pushed me to spend summers on certifications instead of taking the time off. That habit stuck, and it is why I am working through CySA+ now.

Alongside school I spent three years at an EdTech startup, moving from intern to specialist. Most of that job is the governance and communication side of security, which is where I learned how much of this work is people and process rather than tooling. I built this site to keep track of what I have done so far.
