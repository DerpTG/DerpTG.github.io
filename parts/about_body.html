Who I am

I’m a cybersecurity analyst working across security monitoring and incident response, identity and access management, Azure and AWS cloud security, and governance, risk, and compliance. I have also built security programs from the ground up: running a NIST CSF 2.0 gap assessment, writing the policy and controls roadmap, and leading privacy compliance and AI governance work.

Much of my work sits where security meets the business: assessing vendor and third-party risk, reviewing security questionnaires, evaluating cloud and SaaS tools, and turning frameworks like NIST CSF and CIS into controls and training that people actually follow.

I like piecing together what actually happened. That is what moved me from a general computer science start to a cybersecurity degree at Penn State, finished in three years while interning, and it is still the part of the job I like best. This site is where I keep a record of the work, so if any of it is useful to you, reach out.
