Who I am

I went through Penn State as a commuter and finished in three years. Six classes a semester, pulled from campuses across the system, and a fair amount of teaching myself whatever a compressed schedule moved past too quickly.

Security was not the original plan. I came in for computer science broadly and narrowed down once I found the part I kept coming back to, which was working out what happened after the fact. My professors pushed me to spend summers on certifications rather than taking the time off, and that habit is why I am sitting for CySA+ now.

Alongside school I worked my way from intern to specialist at an EdTech startup. The job is mostly governance, compliance, and communication, plus the technical support that keeps everyone running day to day. It taught me that security is a people and process problem at least as often as a technical one. I built this site to keep a record of the work.
